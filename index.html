    Chapter I

    Nobody escalates a Workday problem until it has reached a person.

    By the time a functional area is on your agenda, an employee has usually felt it first. That is not a tooling failure. It is what happens when nothing is watching between releases.

    • 01Payroll is right until the one period it is not.A configuration change three weeks earlier lands on a single pay group, and the first report of it is a person who was paid wrong.
    • 02Open enrolment slips on a file nobody owned.A carrier changes a format, the integration keeps reporting success, and the elections quietly stop arriving.
    • 03An auditor asks who approved something in March.The answer exists somewhere between a ticket, a chat thread, and somebody's memory of a call.
    • 04A release lands on a Saturday.Two functional areas are affected. You find out which on Monday, from the people using them.

    Chapter IV · the estate, one level down

    Every object, and everything that depends on it.

    A read only harvest builds a picture of how your tenant is actually wired: which integrations feed which reports, which domains govern which processes, and what a change to any one of them would touch. It is why an amber on the board can name a cause rather than a symptom.

    • Harvestread only. Nothing is written to your tenant to build the picture.
    • Evidenceevery dependency is marked verified or inferred, and says which.
    • Changesimulated first. Nothing reaches production unsimulated.
    • Recordwho, what, when, and the check that passed, kept as it happens.
